Accessibility

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Draft. This page is written in plain English for a prototype that does not yet handle real customer data. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer and must be before any organization relies on it. Contact details will be added here before real use.

Our target

Pelloria aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA, on this website and in the app.

What has been checked

Every page of this website and every screen of the app has been tested with an automated accessibility checker (axe) against WCAG 2.0 and 2.1 levels A and AA and best practices, in light and dark mode, on desktop and phone widths. The latest results show no violations. In addition:

  • Keyboard: the main app screens can be used with the keyboard alone, every stop shows a visible focus ring, and a "Skip to content" link comes first.
  • Zoom and small screens: pages were checked down to 320 px wide, the equivalent of heavy zoom, with no sideways scrolling of the page.
  • Colour and contrast: text colours meet the required contrast in both themes, and information is never conveyed by colour alone.
  • Motion: animations respect the "reduce motion" setting.
  • Structure: pages use headings, landmarks and labelled controls so assistive technology can navigate them.
  • Errors and offline: if the connection drops, an inline message is shown and what you typed is kept.

What has not been checked yet

  • Manual testing with screen readers such as VoiceOver, NVDA or JAWS. Automated checks cannot confirm the full experience.
  • Testing in Safari and on real phones and tablets.
  • A formal accessibility audit or a conformance report (VPAT).

Because of this, we cannot claim full conformance. Treat this as a good-faith statement of what has been done, not a certification.

Reporting a problem

If something is hard to use, tell us what you were doing, the page, and the browser or assistive technology you use. Contact details will be published here before real use, and accessibility problems will be treated as bugs to fix.
