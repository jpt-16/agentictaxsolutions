The short version

Pelloria helps people collect and check tax data. It does not give tax advice. Its AI agents only make suggestions, a person at your organization reviews and approves every result, and your organization is responsible for its tax positions, filings and decisions. Pelloria is not liable for mistakes in tax.

1. What Pelloria is

Pelloria is software that lets a tax team request data from entities, receive files, have AI agents read and check them, and record a person's approval. It is currently a prototype provided for evaluation, using fictional data. Do not upload real tax, financial or personal data until Pelloria says it is ready for that.

2. No tax, legal or accounting advice

Pelloria does not calculate tax, compute a tax provision, prepare or file returns, or advise on any tax position. Nothing in the software or on this website is tax, legal or accounting advice. Use a qualified professional for that.

3. Human review and responsibility

  • The agents' outputs are suggestions, not conclusions. They can be wrong, incomplete or miss a problem.
  • A person at your organization must review each result against the source before approving it. Nothing is approved automatically.
  • Your organization and the people using Pelloria are solely responsible for what they upload, review, approve, export and file, and for every tax position, return, payment, deadline and decision that relies on data handled in Pelloria.

This applies because all results are reviewed and approved by people at your organization.

Nothing in these terms excludes or limits any liability that cannot be excluded or limited by law.

Pelloria is provided "as is" and "as available". To the fullest extent permitted by law, there are no warranties of any kind, including that it will be accurate, complete, uninterrupted, error-free or fit for a particular purpose.
