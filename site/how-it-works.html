How it works

From a question to an approved dataset, step by step.

Three people take part, and each step is recorded. This is the whole flow.

Step 1 · Tax manager

Create a request

List what you need, such as final post-close trial balances for FY2025. Assign each item to an entity, a department or a person, set a due date, and publish. Reusable templates save retyping the same request every quarter.

Step 2 · Contributor

Upload the files

The person at each entity sees only the items assigned to them. They upload a file with a short note. Files are checked by content, not just by name: disguised programs, scripts and oversized archives are refused with a plain explanation.

Step 3 · Agents

Read and check

The four agents read the file, flag errors and compare it with the other files in the request. Each result shows exactly how it was produced.

Step 4 · Reviewer

Decide on every result

A reviewer approves, edits or rejects each AI result, or sends the item back with a note. If a column was mapped wrongly, they can correct it and rerun. Nothing counts as approved until a person says so.

Step 5 · Everyone

Close and export

When every item is approved, the manager closes the request and exports the approved data as a pack, with a record of who approved each item and when.

Throughout

Work together in the request

Comments, @mentions, deadlines with overdue warnings, notifications, and status reports with CSV export keep the whole team on the same page.

When something is wrong

Problems are surfaced, not buried.

An unsafe or broken file

It is refused at upload, with a reason the contributor can act on.

A number that cannot be read

It is flagged on that row and never silently turned into zero.

The AI is unsure

The result says what it could not determine, and the reviewer decides.
