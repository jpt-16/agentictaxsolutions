In one sentence

Pelloria is where a tax team asks each entity for its numbers, receives the files, has AI agents check them, and records a person's approval of every result.

It handles the data step that comes before the tax calculation. It does not calculate tax, file returns or give tax advice.

The problem

Every quarter, tax teams chase their subsidiaries for numbers.

A global group has dozens of companies, each sending files in its own format, language and currency. The checking is mostly manual.

Files arrive late and messy

Spreadsheets, PDFs and letters in different layouts, with headers like Soll and Haben, in euros, yen and dollars.

Mistakes are found late

A trial balance that does not balance, a duplicated account, or two entities that disagree on a loan. Often found after hours of checking.

Proof is scattered

Auditors and tax authorities ask for evidence months later, and it is spread across inboxes and shared drives.

What Pelloria does

Three steps, with a person in charge of each decision.

  1. 1 · Ask

    A tax manager creates a request: which items, from which entities, due when.

  2. 2 · Upload

    A person at each entity uploads the files against the item. Unsafe or unreadable files are refused with a reason.

  3. 3 · Check and approve

    Four AI agents read the files and flag problems. A reviewer accepts, edits or rejects every result.

  4. 4 · Keep the proof

    Every step is written to an audit log that cannot be quietly changed. Approved data exports as a pack.

Be clear about scope

What it is, and what it is not.

Pelloria does

  • Request data from each entity and track deadlines
  • Read spreadsheets, PDFs and letters, in several languages and currencies
  • Flag errors and mismatches, such as totals that do not balance
  • Compare what different files say about the same thing
  • Record who approved what, and when

Pelloria does not

  • Calculate tax or compute a tax provision
  • File returns with any tax authority
  • Give tax advice or decide anything on its own
  • Replace your provision or compliance software
  • Approve data without a person doing so

Who uses it

Three roles, each with a clear job.

Requester

Tax managers and directors

Create requests, assign them to entities, watch progress and close the request when everything is approved.

Contributor

Finance staff at each entity

See only the items assigned to them, upload files and answer questions. Nothing else is visible to them.

Reviewer

Reviewers and auditors

Review the AI's results against the source, approve or send back, and verify the audit trail independently.
