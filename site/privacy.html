Draft. This page is written in plain English for a prototype that does not yet handle real customer data. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer and must be before any organization relies on it. Contact details will be added here before real use.

The short version

This website does not use accounts, analytics or advertising cookies. The Pelloria app keeps the data needed to run the service for your organization. AI processing is off by default, and sending content to an outside AI provider is a separate switch your administrator controls. Pelloria does not sell data.

1. The website

  • It has no sign-up, no analytics and no advertising or tracking cookies.
  • It loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google's servers, which receive your IP address.
  • Our hosting provider (Vercel) may keep ordinary server logs, such as IP address and pages requested, for security and reliability.

2. The app: what it holds

  • Account details: name, work email, job title, role, and the department or entity you belong to.
  • Content you provide: requests, uploaded files, notes and comments.
  • Results the system produces: extracted data, findings and review decisions.
  • Activity records: an audit log of who did what and when. It is designed so entries cannot be quietly changed or removed, so it is kept as a permanent record.
  • One session cookie that keeps you signed in. It is strictly necessary, and there are no advertising or analytics cookies.

3. Why it is used

Only to provide the service to your organization: showing you your requests, processing your files, recording decisions, and keeping the system secure. Data is not sold, and it is not used for advertising.

4. AI processing

  • AI processing is off by default. An administrator at your organization turns it on.
  • Without an outside model, the agents still work using fixed rules inside Pelloria.
  • Sending file content to an outside AI provider (for example Google Gemini or DeepSeek) is a second, separate switch. If it is on, relevant content from a file is sent to that provider to help read it, after some obvious identifiers are masked. Masking is not a guarantee, so do not turn this on for data you are not comfortable sharing with that provider.
  • Outside AI calls are capped per file, per prompt and per day, and an administrator can set the limit to zero.

5. Who is responsible for the data

Your organization decides what to upload and is responsible for having the right to do so. Pelloria processes that data on your organization's behalf to provide the service.

6. Who can see it

People at your organization, according to their role: contributors see only the items assigned to them, and reviewers and administrators see more. Other organizations cannot see your data, because separation is enforced inside the database itself. Pelloria's operators can access the system to run it. Details are on the Trust page.

7. Where it is stored and for how long

The hosting region and retention periods will be stated here and in your agreement before real use. Today the app runs only with fictional data in development and test environments.

8. Your choices

You can ask your organization's administrator to correct data. Requests for access, correction or deletion of personal data, subject to the permanent audit log described above, will be handled through the contact details that will be published here before real use.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change.
